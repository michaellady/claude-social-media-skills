tell ourselves, not a measurable conversion edge.

## h-2026-05-18-2: Long-form stays the only real subscriber engine
This week's cleanest signal: one long-form (uEposKmbFvY) converted at 20
subs/1K (+3 / 150 views; 30 subs/1K on UNSUBSCRIBED viewers), while the
parallel 24-short burst converted at 0.50 subs/1K. That's a ~40x per-view
gap. h-2026-04-27-7 asked whether shorts at *baseline* volume move subs;
this asks the burst-scale version — does 5x volume change the math? The
prediction is no: by 2026-06-07 long-form subs/1K stays >=10x the burst
rate. If a burst-week of shorts ever closes that gap, the content-mix
strategy should tilt toward shorts; until then, long-form is the sub engine
and shorts are top-funnel.

## h-2026-05-18-3: RELATED_VIDEO is the long-form discovery engine
uEposKmbFvY's traffic mix is suggestion-led, not push-led: RELATED_VIDEO
56.6% (81/150 views), SUBSCRIBER 23.8% (34), NOTIFICATION 1.4% (2), and
69.9% of viewers were unsubscribed. The channel discovers this video
through the suggested-video graph, not its sub base. If that share holds
>50% by 2026-06-07 while the burst runs, the shorts aren't wasted at 0.50
subs/1K — they're seeding the related-video surface that surfaces the
long-form to unsubscribed viewers who then convert at 30 subs/1K UNSUB.
This reframes the burst from "low-converting shorts" to "discovery fuel"
and ties the two formats into one funnel. Refute if RELATED_VIDEO share
falls below 50% (discovery would then be carried by the sub base, and the
shorts' funnel value would be unproven).
